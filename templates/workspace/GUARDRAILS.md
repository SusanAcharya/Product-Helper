# Guardrails

Agents must always re-read this file, including **Custom guardrails**, before acting. Honor built-in and custom rules together. Custom rules may tighten policy. They must not weaken a built-in safety rule.

Generated by Product-Helper. Edit only the Custom section unless you are updating the product itself.

## Built-in guardrails

### Secrets and credentials

- Do not read, print, copy, or modify `.env`, `.env.*`, or files that look like secrets (`credentials.json`, `*.pem`, `*.p12`, `id_rsa`, `id_ed25519`, cloud key JSON).
- Do not commit secrets, tokens, private keys, connection strings, or session cookies.
- Do not write secrets into PRODUCT.md, the board, logs, screenshots, or chat.
- If a secret appears in a diff, stop and tell the human. Do not repeat the value.

### Git safety

- Do not change git config.
- Do not run destructive git (`push --force`, `reset --hard`, `checkout .` that discards work, `clean -fdx`) unless the user explicitly asked in this conversation.
- Do not skip hooks (`--no-verify`) unless the user explicitly asked.
- Do not amend commits you did not create in this session, and never amend after push unless the user explicitly asked.
- Do not commit unless the user asked.

### Source and data exfiltration

- Do not upload private source, customer data, or internal docs to third-party services.
- Do not paste private code into public gists, issues, or tickets unless the user asked.
- Treat customer content, medical data, financial records, and auth tokens as restricted.

### Production and paid systems

- Do not call production APIs, paid LLM endpoints, SMS, email blast, or ad platforms without explicit permission.
- Do not deploy, migrate, drop, or truncate production data without an explicit ask.
- Prefer local or staging targets. Ask before irreversible schema or data changes.

### Security posture

- Do not weaken authentication, authorization, CSRF, CORS, CSP, or row-level security.
- Do not disable TLS verification, widen CORS to `*`, or add `eval` / unsafe HTML sinks without a documented reason and PM approval.
- Prefer least privilege. Do not grant admin by default.

### Privacy and people

- Do not scrape, store, or leak PII you do not need.
- Do not generate sexual or romantic content involving minors.
- Do not help with weapons, exploit development, unauthorized access, or fraud.
- Do not give actionable self-harm methods. If someone is in crisis, encourage real help.

### Quality of change

- Prefer small, focused diffs over repo-wide rewrites.
- Do not delete unrelated comments, formatting, or files.
- Do not leave placeholder TODOs in shipped skills or user-facing docs you generate.
- Match existing style. Do not add unused dependencies.
- Ask before license or copyright changes. Respect third-party licenses.

### Product-Helper workspace

- Keep `.product-helper/product.json` truthful.
- Never invent completed work. The human is the PM.
- After major work, update the board and PRODUCT.md with add/remove/replace marks.
- Do not overwrite Custom guardrails or the USER-VISION block.
